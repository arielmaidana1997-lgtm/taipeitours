Full Description

Begin your journey at the base of the majestic Elephant Mountain. After a scenic hike, you will be rewarded with the most iconic panoramic view of Taipei’s skyline. If it rains, our tour will start at the iconic Taipei 101, where you can admire its bamboo-inspired engineering and have the option to visit the observatory for a stunning bird's-eye view of the city.

Choose from 3 options:

Option 1: The Grand Tour

(Tue – Fri, 13:15)

The most complete immersion into Taipei’s past and present. From the heights, travel by metro to the 228 Peace Memorial Park to explore its poignant history and the grand National Museum of Taiwan, including the fascinating Land Bank Museum. Afterward, walk past the Presidential Office Building into the high-energy district of Ximending. Conclude the day at Dihua Street, the city's oldest commercial heart, ending with the river breeze at Dadaocheng Wharf.

Option 2: Heritage & History

(Sat & Sun, 10:00)

A morning dedicated to the city's cultural foundations. After enjoying the morning views from the mountain or Taipei 101, dive deep into the 228 Peace Memorial Park and its three essential museums. Then, take a scenic walk past the Presidential Office Building to experience the vibrant pop culture of Ximending, finishing your journey among the heritage shops of Dihua Street.

Option 3: Sunset & Night Market

(Sat & Sun, 16:30)

Atmospheric views and Taipei’s world-class food scene. Start with the late afternoon views before heading directly to the historic Dihua Street. Experience the transformation of the city as the sun sets over the Tamsui River at Dadaocheng Wharf. End the night at Ningxia Night Market, where you can immerse yourself in the flavors and aromas of Taipei’s legendary street food.
